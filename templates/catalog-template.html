What the usage-rights badges mean

  • commercial-ok — safe for products, covers, or anything you sell. Double-check the license page anyway; rules can change.
  • share-and-adapt — you may share and modify the work (usually a Creative Commons license). Most require a simple credit line.
  • personal-use-only — keep it in your own projects. Don't sell it or use it in anything commercial without buying a license.
  • check-me — rights are unclear. Look at the license page before using this one for anything beyond personal use.

Plain-English explanations of free-license types live in docs/license-types.md. Print a pocket-size summary from rights-card.html.
